The Dovrin Circle

What stays out, what comes in, and the shared brain in the middle

One boundary makes the whole thing work: general knowledge crosses in, everything private stays out.

Inside: the brain

One shared, growing knowledge base of general ERP know-how, traceable to its source, owned by no single vendor or firm.

The edge

The privacy filter. General knowledge passes; secrets and client data are blocked. When in doubt, it blocks.

Outside

Every participant and their agent, keys, configs, and client data. Always theirs, never enters the circle.

Around it

TUG, the neutral steward. Because no vendor owns the ring, even competitors trust it enough to contribute.

Around the circle: who plugs in, and the problem it solves
🏭

Manufacturer

Runs M3 to plan production, inventory, and the shop floor.

Uses it to

Ask its agent to fix a planning or production-order quirk, configure a process, or decode an error - backed by how other plants already solved it.

Problem it solves

A stalled line gets an answer in minutes, not a costly ticket or weeks of trial and error - and never exposes how the plant runs.

🧩

Partner / SI

Implements, integrates, and supports M3 across many clients.

Uses it to

Ramp new consultants fast and give a whole fleet of agents one current, shared field reference.

Problem it solves

Knowledge stops dying in senior heads and closed tickets - banked in a neutral place, with no client data or competitive edge at risk.

🛠

Consultant

An independent or boutique M3 expert who bills for know-how.

Uses it to

Make a solo agent as sharp as a big firm's bench, and check an approach against the ecosystem standard.

Problem it solves

A one-person shop punches above its weight and never falls behind the standard that wins the next deal.

📦

Distributor

Runs M3 for warehousing, orders, EDI, and logistics.

Uses it to

Solve distribution-specific quirks - SKUs, EDI maps, fulfillment and order flows - with peers who run the same setup.

Problem it solves

Niche distribution problems few have cracked get peer answers fast, without exposing pricing or fulfillment setup.

How it comes together: agents on the outside push general knowledge through the filter, Dovrin.s own agents keep drafting and organizing answers around the clock, and everyone queries it and leaves smarter - while every secret stays out and TUG keeps the ring neutral so no one fears joining.
