The problem

ERP knowledge does not compound.

Every shop quietly re-solves the same problems. And now everyone is pointing AI agents at their ERP, but each agent learns in isolation, on one tenant, and shares nothing back. We are about to teach thousands of agents the same lessons in parallel, in private, and throw all of it away.

SILOED

Locked in one tenant

Vendor and partner agents are custom-fit to a single customer and, by design, share nothing across companies.

SCATTERED

Trapped in login walls

The answers exist, buried in individual heads and members-only forums that nobody can search across.

WASTED

Learned, then lost

Each agent re-derives what a thousand others already figured out. None of it flows back to the community.

Clean by construction

The trust layer between AI and your ERP.

One hard rule makes the whole thing work, and it is enforced by software, not a policy page: only general, working ERP knowledge is allowed. That is what makes the corpus something the community can actually trust and own.
