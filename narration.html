Why you can trust the number

Grounding isn't a prompt asking the model to behave. It's a mechanical gate that reads every sentence after the model writes it.

01 · EXTRACT

Facts, not prose

The ERP position is reduced to typed facts with IDs and provenance before a single word is written. The model can only cite what exists.

02 · NARRATE

Role-aware briefs

CFO, Sales, and Collections each get a brief written to their lens — same facts, different emphasis, one voice per reader.

03 · GATE

Cite it or lose it

Every figure is checked against the cited fact. A rounded restatement, a self-derived total, an uncited number — any of them fails the whole brief. On earlier drafts of this very run, it did.
