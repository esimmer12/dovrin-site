What it does

Not a mockup — a working estimator with real guardrails.

This is the kind of tool Dovrin builds: small in scope, exact in its numbers, and honest about the line it won't let you cross.

Live on every keystroke

Cost, price, and margin recalculate as line items change — no "run the report" step, no stale numbers while a deal is being shaped.

Hard margin-floor guardrails

Any deal that drops below its margin floor is flagged immediately. A hard STOP blocks sign-off outright — it won't quietly let a bad deal through.

Versioned rate cards

Rate cards are versioned, not overwritten. A past estimate never silently restates itself when rates change later — every quote stays pinned to what it was built on.

Client-safe vs. internal exports

One export strips cost and margin for the client's eyes; the other keeps the full internal breakdown. The same estimate, two honest views.

Accurate to the cent

Every total is checked against the source pricing model — no rounding drift, no "close enough." If the model says a number, the estimator matches it exactly.
